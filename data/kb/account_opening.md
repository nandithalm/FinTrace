# Savings account opening documents

To open a simulated savings account in this prototype policy pack you typically need: PAN, Aadhaar, a passport-size photograph, and a completed account-opening form. The bank may ask for more. This is simulated policy, not a real onboarding checklist.

## What do I need to open a savings account?

Bring identity and address proof (PAN and Aadhaar in this pack) plus a photograph. Min KYC versus full KYC is described in the KYC note. Account numbers in FinTrace are always masked (for example XXXXXX4821).
