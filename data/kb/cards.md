# Debit versus credit cards

A debit card in this prototype spends from the linked mock account. A credit card spends against a simulated limit that is billed later. FinTrace cannot hotlist a card or change a PIN. Guidance only: if a debit card is lost, use official bank channels to block it, then request a replacement. This is simulated policy, not a live card-control API.

## How do I report a lost debit card?

Call the bank helpline or use the official app to block the card. Do not share OTP or PIN with anyone, including this prototype. After the block, ask for a reissued card at a branch. FinTrace does not execute the block.
