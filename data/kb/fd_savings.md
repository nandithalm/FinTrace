# How a fixed deposit works

A fixed deposit (FD) in this prototype locks a mock amount for a tenure and earns the simulated FD rate of 6.8% p.a. Premature break rules are not implemented. Savings account interest in the mock table is 3.0% p.a. These strings must match `data/products.json` when Builder 2 lands that file.

## Savings interest (aligned to mock rates)

Savings: 3.0% p.a. Fixed deposit: 6.8% p.a. Personal loan: 11.5% onwards. Home loan: 8.4% onwards. This is simulated product copy, not a live rate sheet.
