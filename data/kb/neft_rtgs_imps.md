# Difference between NEFT and IMPS

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer) in this prototype is a batch credit transfer that is not instant. IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) is treated as near-real-time 24x7 for simulated retail amounts. RTGS is for high-value credits and is not instant in this pack. Exact cut-off times and rupee caps are bank-specific; do not treat these sentences as live RBI limits.

## NEFT versus IMPS (prototype)

Use NEFT when the beneficiary can wait for a batch cycle. Use IMPS when the customer needs the credit to show quickly on a mock statement. UPI is a separate rail and is described in the UPI policy note. This is simulated policy, not a real product checklist.
