# What is KYC?

KYC (Know Your Customer) in this prototype means the bank records identity documents before full account features are enabled. Typical papers are PAN and Aadhaar, plus a photograph. Min KYC is a lighter simulated tier with product limits. Full KYC unlocks the mock savings and loan journeys. This is simulated policy, not a real RBI checklist.

## KYC documents

For this prototype, KYC usually needs: PAN, Aadhaar (or other official ID), and a passport-size photograph. The bank may ask for more. FinTrace will not invent extra document names that are not in this knowledge base.
