# Personal Loan Documents

For this prototype, a personal loan application typically requires:
PAN, Aadhaar, last 3 months salary slips, last 6 months bank statements,
and a passport-size photograph. The bank may ask for more. This is
simulated policy, not a real product checklist.

## Home loan document list

A simulated home loan file typically adds property papers and income proofs on top of PAN and Aadhaar. Exact lists differ by bank. This is simulated policy, not a real product checklist.
