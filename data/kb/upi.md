# UPI limits and PIN

UPI in this prototype policy pack is a simulated instant payment rail. A UPI PIN is used to authorize a payment. FinTrace never displays, resets, or stores a UPI PIN. Collect (pull) requests should be accepted only from known merchants. This is simulated policy, not a live NPCI circular.

## How UPI works

A customer links a bank account to a UPI ID, then sends or receives INR using the UPI app. Credits and debits in this prototype are mock ledger rows. Daily send limits are set by the simulated bank and may differ from a real member bank. Ask a human agent for a live limit.
