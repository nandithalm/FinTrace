# How to raise a transaction dispute

In this prototype, a UPI or card dispute is a support journey, not an automatic reversal. Note the date, merchant, and amount from the mock statement, then raise a ticket through official bank channels. FinTrace will not reverse a debit.

## Dispute steps (simulated)

1. Confirm the row on the simulated transaction list.
2. Keep the merchant name and masked counterparty.
3. Contact the bank helpline or app dispute flow.
4. Do not share PIN, OTP, or password. This is simulated policy, not a live CMS workflow.
